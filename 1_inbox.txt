Inbox Router

Sorts customer emails in 6 languages and drafts the replies
Demo · invented data
For a lender's support team · runs on Google Gemini

Reads each customer email, sorts it by topic and urgency, sends it to the right team and drafts a reply in the customer's language. Fraud, hardship, complaints and data requests always go to a person.
