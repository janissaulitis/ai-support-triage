Inbox Router

Sorts customer emails in 6 languages and drafts the replies
Demo · invented data
For a lender's support team · runs on Google Gemini

Reads each customer email, sorts it by topic and urgency, sends it to the right team and drafts a reply in the customer's language. Fraud, hardship, complaints and data requests always go to a person.

How it works

All emails are invented. AI model: gemini-3.8-flash, then gemini-3.7-flash.

Accuracy test (96 emails)

96 invented emails, each with the answer a careful support lead would give. 16 per language, 2 per topic, including tricky ones: two problems in one email, sarcasm, missing diacritics, a customer who paid a scammer's account. The test checks what matters to the business:

Team: did it reach the right people?
Topic and urgency
Missed risk: a fraud, hardship or regulator threat that slipped through without a person seeing it. This must be 0.
